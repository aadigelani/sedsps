OrbitalGuard: Collision Risk Prioritization

PS-01 · Track 1

Core Question

Among thousands of uncertain collision warnings, which threats actually matter, when should an operator act, and could that action create a new risk?

The Problem

Low Earth Orbit is getting crowded with active satellites, dead spacecraft, rocket bodies, and debris fragments sharing the same orbital paths. Satellites constantly receive collision warnings, but these warnings are uncertain and change as new tracking data comes in.

Operators are stuck between two bad outcomes: overreacting (burning limited fuel, interrupting the mission, shortening satellite life) or underreacting (risking a catastrophic collision). Dodging one collision can even put a satellite on a path toward a different one.

Teams need to turn messy, evolving orbital data into a clear, trustworthy signal for "what to do next."

What You Need to Build

A system that analyzes satellite conjunction/collision warning data and helps operators make better decisions about potential collision risks. It should:

  • Analyze and prioritize potential collision warnings
  • Identify which conjunction events require immediate attention
  • Assign or estimate a risk/confidence level for each event
  • Present results clearly through a dashboard or visualization

Bonus Features (Optional)

  • Temporal risk forecasting
  • Uncertainty estimation
  • Explainable risk scoring
  • Manoeuvre planning
  • Multi-object / cascading conjunction analysis
  • Decision-support simulations

Why It Matters

  • ISRO performed 20 Collision Avoidance Manoeuvres in 2025 and 9 more by August 2026 — this is a routine, ongoing operational burden, not a hypothetical.
  • A single rocket-body fragmentation in June 2026 generated 1,436 collision-risk alerts that month for UK-licensed satellites alone, pushing the tracked object catalogue past 34,751 objects. Risk assessments must update continuously, not be treated as a one-time prediction.

Skills Needed

Time-series forecasting Uncertainty / risk modeling Decision support & optimization
