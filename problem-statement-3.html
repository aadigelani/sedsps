Mission Critical: Satellite Health Under Watch

PS-03 · Track 3

Core Question

Among thousands of satellite telemetry readings, which unusual patterns indicate a potential failure and how severe is the risk?

The Problem

A satellite constantly generates telemetry data across systems such as power, thermal control, orientation, and communications.

Among thousands of readings, some unusual patterns may be early warning signs of potential failures.

What You Need to Build

An AI powered system that analyzes spacecraft telemetry data to:

  • Detect unusual patterns and anomalies in telemetry data
  • Highlight and visualize potential anomalies clearly
  • Assess or assign a risk/severity level to detected anomalies
  • Display overall satellite/system health through an interactive dashboard

Bonus Features (Optional)

  • Identify the likely affected subsystem
  • Suggest possible corrective actions
  • Provide explanations for why an anomaly was flagged
  • Real-time/streaming telemetry simulation
